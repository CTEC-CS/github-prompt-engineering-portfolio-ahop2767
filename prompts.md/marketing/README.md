Design Justification

1. Did you use a specific framework? If yes, which one and why is it appropriate for this use case?
[I used the RFT one because it was the simplest one and I needed something simple for this prompt. The prompt didn’t need to be elaborated just straight to the point.]

2. Why did you structure your prompt this way? What specific elements did you include?
[I specified how the pitch should be because it was being used in a professional setting. I also added the company name since it would help the AI get an idea for what the company would be looking for.]

3. How does your prompt guide the AI effectively?
[My prompt is clear and reusable because it can easily be tweaked for what you need it. The guidance that it is provided is clear enough to guide the AI.]

4. What constraints or requirements did you add and why?
[I noticed that the prompts work better when they are short and direct to the point that the AI understands better. Short and direct prompts are the way to go.]
